Welcome to PantryChef

PantryChef helps you decide what to cook with what you already have, and tells you exactly what to buy for everything else. Or, if you already know what you're making, tick the recipes you're planning to cook to generate one combined shopping list. It's a personal recipe box, a pantry tracker, and a shopping list generator, all in one place.

Quick start

  1. Add recipes on the Add Recipe page - paste a recipe URL, upload a PDF, or type one in by hand.
  2. Keep your Pantry list up to date with what you currently have.
  3. Go to What can I make? to see your recipes ranked by how much you already have.
  4. Pick one or more recipes to generate a shopping list of what's missing.

Features

🔗 Import From a Link or PDF

Paste a recipe URL from hundreds of recipe sites, or upload a PDF you've saved. PantryChef pulls out the ingredients, instructions, photos, prep/cook time, servings, cuisine and course automatically - review and fix anything before it's saved.

🛒 Smart Shopping Lists

Select any combination of recipes and get one combined list, grouped by grocery category. Matching ingredients are summed automatically: 1 egg + 2 eggs = egg (3).

Each item shows which recipe(s) it's for, plus suggested swaps for interchangeable ingredients like sour cream and yogurt.

🥘 What Can I Make?

Ranks your saved recipes by how much of each you already have in your pantry, so you always know what's closest to ready to cook.

📅 Plan a Week

Pick a pool of recipes and PantryChef finds the combinations that share the most ingredients, so a bunch of herbs or a tub of yogurt gets used across several meals instead of a little in each.

🔍 Search & Filters

Find a recipe fast by searching its title or ingredients, or browse by cuisine (Thai, Chinese, Greek...) and meal type (Mains, Salad, Side Dish...), auto-tagged from your imported recipes.

🍳 Cook Mode

Follow along one step at a time with larger, easier-to-read text. Start a timer suggested from the step itself (or set a custom one), and use read-aloud to keep your hands free. Scale ingredients up or down (½×, 2×, 3×) right from the recipe page.

📸 Recipe Details & Photos

Every recipe keeps its photos, source notes and nutrition info, plus prep/cook/total time and servings. Add your own personal notes too - tweaks you made, how it turned out, who liked it.
